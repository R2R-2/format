Kilo Moana TSG file format:
Year DOY Hr Min Sec MSec Stamp Temperature_Internal(°C) Conductivity(µS/cm) Salinity(ppt) Temperature_External(°C) Sound_Velocity(m/s)

Example lines:
2025 354 00 00 08 708 uthsl 25.9809 5.39851 34.9177 25.7203 1536.036
2025 354 00 00 09 708 uthsl 25.9799 5.39843 34.9179 25.7205 1536.037
2025 354 00 00 10 707 uthsl 25.9789 5.39843 34.9186 25.7226 1536.043

Note: some files may have additional gps position fields for latitude and longitude right after the stamp field and/or may lack the sound velocity field
