Kilo Moana Vaisala WXT536 Data File Field Description:

        1: Year
        2: Ordinal Day (day of year)
        3: Hour
        4: Minute
        5: Second
        6: MSeconds
        7: wxt536 (sensor)
        8: True Wind Speed (knots) - Calculated from posmv and wxt
        9: True Wind Direction (deg) - Calculated from posmv and wxt
        10: Heading (deg) - From posmv
        11: Speed Over Ground (knots) - From posmv
        12: Course Over Ground (deg) - From posmv
        13: Relative Wind Direction (deg) - From wxt
        14: Relative Wind Speed (knots) - From wxt
        15: Air Temperature (deg C) - From wxt
        16: Relative Humidity (%) - From wxt
        17: Barometric pressure (mbar) - From wxt
        18: Rain Accumulation (mm) - From wxt
        19: Rain Intensity (mm/min) - From wxt

Note: Any fields that require data from GPS will show "00" if no GPS data was available at that time
Note: Fields from posmv come from Applanix POS/MV-320 V4 INS and fields from wxt come from Vaisala WXT536 metstation
