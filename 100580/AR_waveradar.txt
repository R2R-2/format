Neil Armstrong Wave Radar Format

Fields: 
	1.  date
	2.  time (UTC) 
	3.  longitude (deg E) 
	4.  latitude (deg N)
	5.  peak wave period (s)
	6.  mean wave period (s) 
	7.  peak wave direction (deg CWFN coming from)
	8.  significant wave height (m)
	9.  near-surface current speed (m/s) 
	10. near-surface current direction (deg CWFN going to)
