Atlantis Seametrics SPX-038 Flowmeter Data Format

Raw Data Format:
System Timestamp Type Counts/Second

Worked Example:
SSW 2026-04-25T04:05:53.777Z FLOW 11

SSW				Surface Sea Water
2026-04-25T04:05:53.777Z	Timestamp (GMT)
FLOW 				Flowmeter Data
11 				Pulse Counts Per Second

Example Data Lines:
SSW 2026-04-25T04:05:53.777Z FLOW 11
SSW 2026-04-25T04:05:54.833Z FLOW 11
SSW 2026-04-25T04:05:55.889Z FLOW 10
SSW 2026-04-25T04:05:56.945Z FLOW 11
SSW 2026-04-25T04:05:58.001Z FLOW 11
